# Beauty Heaven Hub — privacy notice (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT for review — not legal advice.** Good Work isn't a law firm. This is a
> plain-English starting point that describes how the website, the assistant and
> the CRM actually handle data, so a solicitor can check and finish it before it
> goes live. The **open points** at the bottom need a lawyer's eye in
> particular. Nothing here is published to the site yet.

## The notice (website copy)

**Who we are**
Beauty Heaven Hub LTD (registered in England and Wales, company number
15072077) is responsible for the personal information you give us. You can reach
us at **halo@beautyheavenhub.co** or 23–24 Conduit Lane, Hoddesdon,
Hertfordshire, EN11 8FN.

**What we collect, why, and our lawful basis**

- **When you book a treatment** — your name, mobile, email (optional) and whether
  you want marketing. We use it to make and manage your appointment. *Lawful
  basis: to carry out our contract with you; marketing by consent.* Bookings are
  held in our booking system, **Phorest**.
- **When you make an enquiry** — on a form, by email, or through our assistant —
  your name, contact details, what you're asking about and your message. We use
  it to reply and follow up. *Lawful basis: your consent, and our legitimate
  interest in responding.* Enquiries are held in our own CRM, **Twenty**.
- **When you message our assistant** (on WhatsApp, Telegram or the site) — it is
  an **AI assistant**, and it tells you so. We keep a record of the conversation
  so the team can help you and so we have a record of what was agreed. *Lawful
  basis: our legitimate interest in running the service; consent for marketing.*
- **When you call** — your call may be forwarded so we don't miss it. We don't
  record calls.
- **Health and suitability information** — for some treatments we ask about your
  health, medication or suitability. This is collected on the relevant
  consultation and consent forms and is seen only by the practitioner treating
  you. *Lawful basis: your explicit consent; it is never put into our CRM or the
  assistant.*
- **Reviews** — if you leave a Google review it's handled by Google under its own
  policy.

**Marketing**
We only send offers and news if you've asked us to. You can stop any time — tell
us, or use the unsubscribe link.

**Who we share it with**
We don't sell your information. We use trusted services to run ours, and they
only act on our instructions: **Phorest** (bookings), our own **Twenty** CRM
(enquiries), **Supabase** (securely storing the assistant's conversations),
**Vercel** (hosting the website), and **Anthropic** (the AI that powers the
assistant — your message is sent to it to work out a reply). If you message on
**WhatsApp** or **Telegram**, that platform handles the message too.

Our practitioners are self-employed. Where one treats you, they keep their own
treatment records and are responsible for them.

**Sending data outside the UK**
Some of these services process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on
approved safeguards to keep it protected. *[Solicitor to confirm wording — see
open points.]*

**How long we keep it**
We keep booking and treatment records for as long as we need them for the
service and the law, and enquiry records only while they're useful, then delete
them. *[Retention periods to be set — see open points.]*

**Your rights**
You can ask us to show you the information we hold, correct it, delete it,
restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy. You can withdraw consent
at any time. To do any of these, email **halo@beautyheavenhub.co**. If you're not
happy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

**Cookies**
This website doesn't use advertising cookies — only what it needs to work.

*Last updated: [date on publishing].*

## Open points for the solicitor

1. **Health data (special category).** Confirm the lawful basis (explicit
   consent) and the wording for consultation/consent forms, and that it stays out
   of the CRM and the assistant.
2. **Self-employed practitioners.** Confirm who is controller for treatment
   records — the salon, the practitioner, or jointly — and reflect it. This drives
   the data agreement too.
3. **International transfers.** Anthropic (and possibly others) process data in
   the US. Confirm the safeguard wording (e.g. the UK addendum to the EU SCCs).
4. **Retention periods.** Set concrete periods: bookings/treatment records,
   enquiry/CRM leads, and assistant transcripts (the code starts a fresh chat
   after 12h, but stored history persists — propose a deletion period, e.g. 12
   months).
5. **AI disclosure + processing.** Confirm the notice adequately explains that
   messages are sent to an AI model.
6. **The data agreement** between Good Work (processor) and Beauty Heaven Hub
   (controller) — the companion to this notice.
