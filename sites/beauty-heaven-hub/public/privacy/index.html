Who we are

Beauty Heaven Hub LTD, registered in England and Wales, company number 15072077 is responsible for the personal information you give us. You can reach us at halo@beautyheavenhub.co or 23-24 Conduit Lane, Hoddesdon, Hertfordshire, EN11 8FN.

What we collect, why, and our lawful basis

  • When you book a treatment — your name, mobile, email (optional) and whether you want marketing. We use it to make and manage your appointment. Lawful basis: to carry out our contract with you; marketing by consent. Bookings are held in our booking system, Phorest.
  • When you make an enquiry — on a form, by email, or through our assistant — your name, contact details, what you're asking about and your message. We use it to reply and follow up. Lawful basis: your consent, and our legitimate interest in responding.
  • When you message our assistant — it is an AI assistant, and it tells you so. We keep a record of the conversation so the team can help you and so we have a record of what was agreed. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in running the service; consent for marketing.
  • When you call — your call may be forwarded so we don't miss it. We don't record calls.
  • Health and suitability information — for some treatments we ask about your health, medication or suitability. This is collected on the relevant consultation and consent forms and is seen only by the practitioner treating you. Lawful basis: your explicit consent.
  • Reviews — if you leave a Google review it's handled by Google under its own policy.

Marketing

We only send offers and news if you've asked us to. You can stop any time — tell us, or use the unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We use trusted services to run ours, and they only act on our instructions: Phorest (bookings), our own CRM (enquiries), and the services that host the website and power the assistant. If you message on WhatsApp or Telegram, that platform handles the message too. Our practitioners are self-employed; where one treats you, they keep their own treatment records and are responsible for them.

Sending data outside the UK

Some of these services process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on approved safeguards to keep it protected.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and treatment records for as long as we need them for the service and the law, and enquiry records only while they're useful, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive a copy. You can withdraw consent at any time. To do any of these, email halo@beautyheavenhub.co. If you're not happy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising cookies — only what it needs to work.
