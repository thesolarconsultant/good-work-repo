How we build

The finish matters. So does everything underneath it.

Most driveway problems — sinking, pooling, weeds through the joints — start below the surface. This is the build-up we work to on a standard domestic drive.

Depths and specification vary with ground conditions, access and drainage. Your free site survey confirms what your property actually needs.

  1. 01ExcavationDig out to the depth the finished level and ground conditions require, and cart the spoil away.
  2. 02Type 1 sub-baseClean crushed hardcore laid in layers — the part that actually carries the vehicle.
  3. 03CompactionEach layer compacted properly. Skip this and the surface tells on you within a winter.
  4. 04Edging & drainageHaunched edge restraints and falls set so water leaves the drive where it should.
  5. 05Laying course & surfaceScreeded bed, then your chosen finish — block, resin, tarmac or stone — cut in and jointed.
