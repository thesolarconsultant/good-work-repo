What should Beauty Heaven keep renting — and what would eventually make more sense to own?

Phorest

It does a real job. This is not a teardown.

Route one

Keep & connect

Phorest stays the operational engine. We build better experiences around it and make it talk to everything else.

Route two

Augment

Keep what Phorest is good at. Build owned Beauty Heaven technology only where you need flexibility it can't give you.

Route three

Eventually replace

Build an owned operational platform — but only if the commercial and technical case genuinely justifies it.

Before recommending any of these we would need to assess data accessibility and export routes, integration and API capability, booking and staff workflows, customer history, communication requirements, existing dependencies and real commercial costs.

Any future migration strategy would prioritise preserving Beauty Heaven's historical customer and operational data — subject to confirming the available export routes, data access and technical migration feasibility.

Renting isn't bad. Owning isn't automatically better. The business case decides.

This is the part that matters

Before we build anything, we need to know what you actually want.

  • What does the current technology stack actually cost?
  • Which systems genuinely cause problems?
  • Which ones work, and should be left alone?
  • What would you like to own?
  • Would an Academy platform be only for you?
  • Or would you want other academies on it?
  • Is the marketplace an interesting idea — or a business?
  • How involved would you each want to be?
  • What appetite is there for investment?
  • Where should Beauty Heaven be in twelve months?

There are no right answers here, and we are not trying to steer you towards the biggest one. We just can't scope any of it until we know.
