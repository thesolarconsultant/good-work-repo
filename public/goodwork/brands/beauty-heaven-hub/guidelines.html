01

the brand idea.

Multiple beauty disciplines and professional education, under one roof. A place people travel to.

The feeling: premium, warm, established, expert, welcoming. Feminine without being girly.

The reviews already describe it. Customers call the team professional, friendly and reassuring, and talk about feeling comfortable and at ease. The brand's job is to look the way that sounds.

02

the logo.

The existing logo stays at the heart of the identity. Three words, one size. The only distinction is weight.

beauty heaven hublightboldlight

Don't recreate it with sizes

The weight change is the whole idea. Never enlarge "heaven"; never shrink "hub".

Restraint is the point

  • No gradients in the logo
  • No drop shadows
  • No fake metallic effects everywhere
  • No pink version

One sanctioned light effect

On a dark or taupe ground the gold may catch the light: a single sweep across the letterforms every six and a half seconds, then stillness — as above, and in the closing band of the site. The gold underneath stays flat. It is light moving over the mark, never a gradient baked into it, and it stops under reduced motion. Print never gets it.

Clear space

The height of the lowercase b on every side. The ground-colour files have it built in.

Minimum size

Wordmark: 120px on screen, 30mm in print. Below that, use the stacked mark.

The master

The supplied logo is logo/original.png and is the master for print. The SVGs alongside it are rebuilt from the typeface to match, for lockups the original doesn't cover.

04

the type.

Jost. One geometric family that mirrors the logo: single-storey a, straight y, a bold with real width. Clean, architectural, spacious.

No high-fashion serif just because it's a beauty company. The premises and the logo are more contemporary than that. Headlines are lowercase for brand and editorial moments; large uppercase is allowed for major statements.

physical → digital.

The most important rule. We take the website design from the building, so it feels like both were designed at the same time.

06

the heaven language.

The name earns a visual vocabulary: halo, light, glow, wings, reveal, ascension, soft atmospheric transitions. It has to stay sophisticated.

The drawn line. One fine gold hairline that paints itself as you scroll, each leaf opening exactly as the line reaches it. Generated rather than drawn by hand, so every leaf sits on the stem's own tangent at its own angle and size — copy-pasted leaves read as clip art immediately.

One plant per section at most. Always at an edge or behind a heading, never behind body copy. Gold hairline only: no fills, no second colour, no shadow. Decorative, so hidden from screen readers, and simply drawn under reduced motion.

Yes

  • A very subtle halo behind a treatment image
  • Circular light moving as someone scrolls
  • The actual wings from the lounge, filmed properly
  • Text rising into view through an arch

No

  • Clouds
  • Angels flying around the website
  • Glitter
  • Sparkly beauty-template nonsense

08

the motion.

Slow, smooth and expensive. Not "look how many animations we can add." The site should feel like moving through the building.

  • Slow cinematic reveals, 1.2 to 1.4 seconds
  • Masked image transitions through the arch
  • Gentle parallax; the halo drifting with the scroll
  • Text emerging through architectural shapes
  • Smooth page transitions
  • Subtle image scaling on hover, 1.03 and never more
  • Horizontal editorial galleries
  • A gold hairline drawing in under a nav link

Three rules

Everything is transform and opacity only. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion. Buttons are pills, and the fork is two arches: no section numbers, no numbered steps, anywhere on the site.

12

social proof.

Reviews aren't a widget at the bottom. They're brand material.

Loved by hundreds of clients.

A recurring trust device: in the hero, on the reviews wall, on print. Then real customer comments appear contextually. A long-standing client's words about Hollie and Jessica's care, professionalism and precision belong beside their portraits, not in a carousel. A nervous first-timer's review belongs next to the reassurance copy on the treatments page.

Before launch: replace the paraphrased quotes in the prototype with verbatim reviews, with permission.
