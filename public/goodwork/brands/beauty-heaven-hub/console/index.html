      Where this is kept.

      Everything you write here — the brand, the ideas, the content — is stored in this browser, on this device. It is not on a server, it is not in anyone's account, and it will not follow you to your phone or to anyone else on the team.

      That is a real limit and it is the next thing to fix. It needs a proper database and logins before more than one person can use it, and that is a decision with a cost attached rather than something to quietly assume.

      In the meantime nothing here is trapped. The button below gives you the lot as a file. Keep it somewhere, and it loads straight back in.

      Writing runs through /api/console on Good Work's deployment, which holds the API key. The key is never in this page. If the console says it cannot write, that endpoint has no key set.
