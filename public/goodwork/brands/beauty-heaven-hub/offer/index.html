The part worth understanding

The chatbot has a second job.

Answering enquiries is the obvious half. The other half is why the console never runs dry.

Content dies because on a Monday morning nobody can think of anything to say. Not because nobody can write — because nobody can remember what happened.

So the bot asks. Once a week it puts a question to you and Hollie — what came up this week, what did people keep asking, what went well. Thirty seconds on WhatsApp, in the place you already are. Spread it across the team and you have the whole floor feeding it, not just whoever remembered.

That, plus the questions real customers asked the bot, plus what the phone agent heard, goes straight into the Content Console through an API link. Your ideas board fills itself with the things people actually want to know.

Nobody starts from a blank page again. That is the whole point of building it as one system rather than four separate ones.

A note on text messages.

SMS is a different case, and worth being straight about. Text messaging currently runs inside Phorest, and it is Phorest that sends it. We can design and write what goes out — but for us to run SMS campaigns ourselves, a dedicated SMS system would have to sit alongside Phorest. That is its own piece of work, and only worth doing if you decide you want it.

Where the line sits.

The platform fee keeps that infrastructure and those templates maintained. It does not include running campaigns through them. When you want more

The same agent can call out, not just pick up.

The voice agent in the build answers the phone. The same agent works the other way too — ringing people rather than waiting on them. A client who has not been in for eight months.
