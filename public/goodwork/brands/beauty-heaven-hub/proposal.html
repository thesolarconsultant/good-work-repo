The short version

we are not here to rebrand you.

You have already started building the brand. It is in the arches, the fluted doors, the champagne finishes and the taupe walls. It just has not been written down, and the website does not know about any of it yet.

So this is not a rebrand. It is codifying what the premises already says, and then building a website that could only belong to Beauty Heaven Hub — because every decision in it comes out of the room you are standing in.

The method

the website comes from the building.

Every rule below is a real feature of the premises turned into a real behaviour on screen. Nothing here was invented to look good in a deck.

→
Arched photography containers and masks. Every image on the site is cropped to the same curve.
Champagne and brushed gold

Fine gold hairlines, icon strokes and micro-interactions. Line weight, never fill.
Dark fluted panelling

A subtle vertical texture on the dark editorial sections — the one you are reading over now.

One thing to settle early: the purple lighting in the treatment rooms is environmental, not brand. It photographs beautifully and it should stay in the photography — but Beauty Heaven is not a purple brand, and the site should not become one.

Typography

the logo is already the type rule.

beautyheavenhub

Three words, one size. The difference is weight — light, bold, light. That single detail is the whole typographic system, and it is why the wordmark should never be rebuilt with different sizes.

So the site uses a modern geometric sans throughout: clean, architectural and spacious, to match the premises rather than fight it. Headlines sit in lowercase. Bold is used the way the logo uses it — for one word, on purpose.

No high-fashion serif. It is the reflex for a beauty brand and it would make you look like every other salon, when the building already looks like none of them.

The idea worth building the site around

what are you here for?

Two very different people arrive at this website. One wants something for themselves today. One is deciding what to do with their career. Asking them directly, on arrival, is stronger than a menu that makes them work it out.

From that one question, the whole journey personalises — different navigation, different proof, different next step. Compare it to HOME | ABOUT | SERVICES | COURSES | CONTACT, which asks the visitor to do the sorting for you. The two above are a demonstration — hover or tab to them to feel it.

Motion & voice

it should feel like moving through the building.

Yes
  • Slow cinematic reveals, and images masked into arches as they arrive
  • Gentle parallax, and text emerging through architectural shapes
  • Circular light moving as someone scrolls
  • Horizontal editorial galleries, and hover states worth hovering
No
  • Clouds, floating angels, glitter
  • Sparkly beauty-template effects
  • Animation counted by the number of animations
  • Anything that arrives faster than the room deserves
The voice

“We will explain exactly what happens, and you can ask anything.”

Confident, warm, knowledgeable, straightforward, reassuring. Your reviews already say this is how the team actually is — nervous first-timers repeatedly mention being put at ease. The website should sound like the people in the building.

Not this

“Indulge in an exquisite sanctuary of transformative beauty…”

Says nothing, reassures nobody, and reads as a template. Premium does not mean distant.
